# Status: Digital Omnibus on AI

As of 2026-11-26. Checked against EUR-Lex.

## The act

- **Regulation (EU) 2026/1744** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 July 2026
  amending Regulations (EU) 2024/1689, (EU) 2018/1139 and (EU) 2023/1230 as regards the
  simplification of the implementation of harmonised rules on artificial intelligence
  (Digital Omnibus on AI).

  ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj
- Date of effect: **27 July 2026**.

## Consequences for the assistant

- Answers about high-risk obligations use the consolidated text. An answer that names
  2 August 2026 for Annex III systems is wrong for any reference date after 27 July 2026.
- The new prohibitions in Article 5(1)(ba), (bb), 5(1a) and (1b) apply from 2 December 2026.
  Questions about them depend on the reference date.
